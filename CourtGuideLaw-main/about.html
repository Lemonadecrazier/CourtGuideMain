ABOUT COURTGUIDE

Making court information easier to understand.

CourtGuide is a simple educational resource designed to help people understand common court roles, terminology, and processes.

OUR PURPOSE

Court language can be difficult. We want to make it clearer.

CourtGuide was created to organize common legal terminology and court concepts into information that is easier to navigate.

Instead of presenting everything as one large block of legal language, CourtGuide breaks information into simple categories and interactive sections.

The goal is to help visitors understand unfamiliar terms, identify useful questions, and better understand topics they may want to discuss with a qualified legal professional.

WHO IS IT FOR?

CourtGuide is designed for everyone.

Different people can have very different roles in a court case. These are some of the people who may find the information useful.

01

Plaintiffs

People bringing a civil claim and seeking to understand the general process.

02

Defendants

People responding to a legal claim or accusation.

03

Witnesses

People who may provide information or testimony about a case.

04

Supporters

Family members or others helping someone involved in a case.

HOW IT WORKS

Learn. Prepare. Verify.

CourtGuide is intended to be a starting point for understanding unfamiliar court information.

STEP 01

Learn

Understand common court terminology, roles, and general processes.

STEP 02

Prepare

Organize questions, documents, dates, and information relevant to your situation.

STEP 03

Verify

Confirm important information with official court resources or qualified legal professionals.

Important disclaimer

CourtGuide provides general educational information and does not provide legal advice.

Laws and court procedures vary by jurisdiction. Information on this website should not be used as a substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer or official court resource.
